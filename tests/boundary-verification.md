## LITO (Low Income Tax Offset)

- Max: $700 (for income ≤ $37,500)
- Phase-out 1: $37,501–$45,000 → reduces by 5c per $1 over $37,500
- Phase-out 2: $45,001–$66,667 → reduces by 1.5c per $1 over $45,000
- Zero above $66,667

| Taxable Income | Calculation | LITO |
|---|---|---|
| $0 | Max offset | $700.00 |
| $37,500 | Max offset | $700.00 |
| $37,501 | $700 − ($1 × 0.05) | $699.95 |
| $40,000 | $700 − ($2,500 × 0.05) = $700 − $125 | $575.00 |
| $45,000 | $700 − ($7,500 × 0.05) = $700 − $375 | $325.00 |
| $45,001 | $325 − ($1 × 0.015) | $324.985 |

| $100,000 | Above phase-out | $0.00 |
